## Full description

Professional of Badminton is the reservation app for the academy's open play
sessions in Khalda and Shmeisani.

Stop asking on WhatsApp whether there is a game tonight. Open the app.

**See what is on.** Every session for the next five days, with the venue, the
time, the price and how many spots are left.

**Reserve in two taps.** Pay cash at the venue, send a CliQ transfer and attach
the screenshot, or use a credit from your subscription.

**Know where your credits went.** Every credit you have, when it expires, and
the history of every one you have used.

**Hear when a spot opens.** Join the waiting list on a full session and the app
tells you the moment somebody cancels.

**Read the coach's announcements**, in Arabic or English, whichever he writes
in.

The app is in Arabic and English, and switches between them whenever you like.

The coach still runs the sessions. The app only saves everyone the messages.
