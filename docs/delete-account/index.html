Delete your account — Professional of Badminton

Last updated: 1 September 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Professional of Badminton account and the data attached to it, and exactly what is removed and what is kept.

How to delete your account from the app

  1. Open the Professional of Badminton app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Type the confirmation word you are shown, because this cannot be undone.

The account is deleted immediately. There is no waiting period and no further confirmation by email.

If you cannot open the app

If you have lost access to your account or removed the app, message the academy on WhatsApp at +962 79 284 1696 from the phone number on the account and ask for the account to be deleted. We will answer within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your name, email address and phone number are removed from our records.
  • Your notification tokens and your CliQ transfer screenshots are deleted.
  • Your sign-in is destroyed. You cannot sign in again with that account.
  • Every reservation you hold for a future session is cancelled, and any credit those reservations used is returned.

What is kept, and for how long

DataKeptWhy
Past reservations and payment recordsKept indefinitely, without your name attachedSo the academy's accounts for past months still add up
CliQ transfer screenshotsDeleted with the account. Otherwise deleted automatically 365 days after uploadLong enough for any dispute about a transfer, and no longer
Crash reports (Sentry)Contain no name, email, phone number or screen contentsNothing personal to delete

Deleting your account does not settle money you owe the coach, and does not create money owed to you. That remains between you and him.

See also the privacy policy.
