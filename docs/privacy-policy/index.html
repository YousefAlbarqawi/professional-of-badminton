Privacy Policy — Professional of Badminton

Last updated: 21 August 2026

Professional of Badminton is a reservation app for a single badminton academy in Amman, Jordan. This policy describes exactly what the app collects, why, who can see it, and how to have it removed.

We collect the minimum needed to run the sessions. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no advertising identifier of any kind.

What we collect

When you create an account

DataWhy
First and last nameSo the coach knows who has reserved a spot
Email addressTo sign you in, and to confirm your account
Phone numberSo the coach can reach you about a session
PasswordStored only as a cryptographic hash. Nobody, including the coach, can read it
Preferred languageSo the app opens in Arabic or English as you chose

When you use the app

DataWhy
Your reservations, and the payment method you choseTo hold your spot and to settle the session afterwards
CliQ transfer screenshots you attachSo the coach can confirm the transfer arrived
Amounts paid, and any amount outstandingThe coach's record of the session
Subscription credits and their historySo you can see where your credits went
Your skill level, set by the coachTo arrange the courts
A notification token, if you allow notificationsTo tell you when a spot opens, and when the coach posts an announcement

When something goes wrong

If the app crashes, a crash report is sent to Sentry. It contains the technical details of the failure — what code failed and on which model of phone. It does not contain your name, your email, your phone number, a screenshot of what you were looking at, or the contents of any screen.

What we do not collect

  • Location, at any time
  • Contacts, calendar, microphone, or any photo other than a CliQ screenshot you choose to attach
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA, GAID) — the app requests none and contains no advertising SDK
  • Analytics of any kind. There is no analytics SDK in the app
  • Your activity anywhere outside this app

Who can see it

  • You see your own reservations, your own credits, and your own details.
  • The coach and the academy's admins see the reservation list, payments, balances and skill levels. This is the academy's own record-keeping.
  • Other players see only what your visibility setting allows: nothing about other attendees, or their skill levels, or their skill levels and names. The coach sets this. It never reveals a phone number or an email address to another player.

Who processes it for us

These providers hold data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes. Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared for advertising.

ProviderWhat it holdsWhere
SupabaseThe database, the account records, and the CliQ screenshotsCloud hosting
Expo push service (Expo / Apple APNs / Google FCM)The notification token and the text of a notification, in transitCloud service
SentryCrash reports, with no personal data in themCloud service

How long we keep it

  • CliQ screenshots are deleted automatically 365 days after upload. Long enough for any dispute about a transfer, and no longer.
  • Reservations, payments and credit history are kept as the academy's financial record.
  • Your account is kept until you delete it.

Deleting your account

Open the app, go to Profile, and tap Delete my account. You will be asked to type a word to confirm, because it cannot be undone.

When you delete your account:

  • Every reservation you hold for a future session is cancelled, and any credit those reservations used is returned.
  • Your name, email address and phone number are removed from our records.
  • Your notification tokens and your CliQ screenshots are deleted.
  • Your sign-in is destroyed. You cannot sign in again with that account.
  • Past reservations and payment records remain, without your name attached, so the academy's accounts for past months still add up.

Deleting your account does not settle money you owe the coach, and does not create money owed to you. That remains between you and him.

Your rights

Write to the academy on WhatsApp — the number is in the app on almost every screen — to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. We will answer within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly create accounts for them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top and post an announcement in the app.

Contact

حذف الحساب لا يسوّي مبلغًا مستحقًا عليك للكابتن، ولا ينشئ مبلغًا مستحقًا لك. يبقى ذلك بينك وبينه.
